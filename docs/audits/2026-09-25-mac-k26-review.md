  d1dc4c175ea2518a534d67d2855dfe5c68e03debd0a3f1c9672da788c0edebb3.

  The new instructions supply the complete command, tool source, and the prehash distinction.

  Identity binding, time anchoring, matching measurements, and runtime behavior are distinct claims.
  The previous forecast that moving to the Mac automatically improved claim 3 was too strong.
* No evidence supports the report's sentence that plaintext left the client despite degradation.
  plaintext_bytes_sealed_here is cumulative input to encryption, not transmitted plaintext; the

## Review of the completed 0.9.54 audit

The Mac run completed with `ir --plain --model kimi-k2.6` requested, process exit 0, and a report. Its actual served model was not independently recorded. Preserve the original unchanged as `2026-09-26-mac-k26-0954-original.md`; do not use it as a clean audit verdict.

**The report fails the brief's required claim map.** It wrote its own headings and shifted claims: its claim 2 is recipient sealing (the brief's claim 2 is hardware binding of the statement-signing key); claim 5 is filters (brief claim 5 is completeness/no removal); claim 6 is AI conversation (brief claim 6 is filter application); claim 7 is a subset of the brief's claim 7; and claim 8 changes the wording and meaning. The trace contains no read of `REPORT-TEMPLATE.md`, despite instructions to complete it. Therefore its table is not a valid verdict table.

**Two substantive conclusions are contradicted by the brief and evidence.** Claim 8 is marked VERIFIED despite `document_reads: 0`; the brief explicitly says that means COULD NOT CHECK. The closing paragraph says no party outside the enclave was positioned to read the text, although the policy reports `allow_stdio_access: true` and an imported fragment and the confidentiality block does not license that sentence. It also calls it InferRoute's enclave after giving claim 3 only VERIFIED IN PART, which violates the brief's explicit closing restriction. These errors make the report unsafe to quote.

**Other report statements are incomplete or too strong.** The report says the OTS proof and Sigstore bundle could not be checked because the tools were absent, but the trace shows no attempt to install the documented `opentimestamps-client` or `cosign`; an unsuccessful `ots verify --no-bitcoin` was attempted. The TDX hash comparison establishes that bytes in a self-reported receipt match a quote field; without verifying the TDX quote signature/chain, it does not establish that a trusted enclave committed to the key. The report itself acknowledges that gap, then states the commitment as fact. Its filter distribution and manual Ed25519/AMD chain checks are useful observations, but they do not repair the misnumbered claims or the invalid closing statement.

The verifer's exit 0 is only its cryptographic search-record checks. It is not an audit-wide pass and does not validate the report's claims or confidentiality prose. The report author said they had not read neighboring reports; this review compared it only with the brief and the launch trace, not with any prior audit report.
